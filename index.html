The Peacekeeper

When the courage you need is greater than the strength you have left.

Eddie Pirini, a lizard-shifter, faces an unplanned pregnancy while his human girlfriend, Ainsley, struggles to accept his animal form. A therian crime syndicate, the Fraksiya, is operating in Redcliffe and new Special Forces agents are arriving. How can he be The Peacekeeper, when everything is falling apart?
